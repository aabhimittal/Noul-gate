What the guarantee does and does not mean

  • Marginal guarantee. The bound says: averaged over harmful actions like those in the calibration set (and over the draw of that set), at most α are allowed. It says nothing about any single action.
  • Exchangeability. It assumes deployment harmful actions look like calibration harmful actions. New attack styles, new tools or a new agent model break that assumption.
  • Adaptive attackers. An attacker who can probe the gate can craft text that lowers p(x); the bound does not hold against them. Treat α as a monitoring target, not a proof of safety, and recalibrate on fresh attack data.
  • Labels. "Harmful" is a judgment call; label noise and annotator disagreement cap what any backend can reach.
